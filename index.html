Founding Sponsorship & Partnership Opportunities

Connecting Arkansas with the future of emerging technology, nationally and globally.

Arkansas Blockchain Association brings together business leaders, technology professionals, universities, students, and public sector stakeholders to advance practical understanding and responsible adoption of artificial intelligence (AI), blockchain, digital assets, quantum computing, and emerging technologies.

We invite founding sponsors to help build the programs, connections, and infrastructure that make this work possible.

What ABA is building

Practical programs. Connected thinking.

ABA is building a network for practical knowledge, collaboration across sectors, and responsible innovation, grounded in practical applications.

ABA is developing masterclasses, executive briefings, educational materials, and discussions to help people understand emerging technologies and their practical applications.
ABA is developing working groups, roundtables, collaborative projects, and connections to bring together businesses, universities, and technology organizations.
ABA plans to support informed discussions and resources addressing ethics, governance, legislation, and regulation.
ABA plans member events, industry panels, summits, and participation in relevant national and international forums to bring useful insights and connections back to ABA’s community.
Interactive knowledge, contextual AI guidance, and blockchain enabled experiences are being developed through ABA’s technology partnership with Center for Creators.

Areas of interest: Enterprise applications • Digital finance • Supply chain and logistics • Workforce development • Government applications • Smart cities • Arts and culture
